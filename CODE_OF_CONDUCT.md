# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive and professional community.

Participants should communicate constructively, respect different experience levels, protect confidential information and focus criticism on ideas and behaviour rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate disclosure of private information and disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct is inconsistent with these expectations. Reports should be made privately to the maintainers through an appropriate private GitHub channel. Maintainers will review reports fairly and protect the reporter's privacy as far as practical.
