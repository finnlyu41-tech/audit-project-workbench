# Simple business modules / 简化业务模块

In an annual company engagement, choose a business module and open **Configure selected workstream → Workstream mode → Simple mode**. New modules offer the same choice. Each module chooses its own mode; full and simple modules can coexist in one annual engagement.

Simple mode manages the whole module with **Not started, In progress, On hold, Completed**, an owner, start date, due date and literal notes. There are no stages, completion criteria or nested tasks. Change status directly in the module panel; use **Edit workstream details** for its other fields. Module dates and owner do not change the annual engagement's schedule or owner.

Switching an existing module to simple mode retains its original node IDs, conditions and checks for a later switch back. Its simple status is independent and initially Not started; no completion is inferred from old nodes. Retained nodes are excluded from active progress and next-step suggestions. A new simple module does not instantiate a selected template's nodes.

Only Completed counts as a completed simple module. Its card and record report display status rather than a fabricated intermediate percentage. Existing detailed modules keep their criterion-based calculation; combined project progress counts each simple module as one completion unit alongside the active criteria of detailed modules. A simple module contributes zero until completed and one when completed. Outstanding items remain separate and are never automatically cleared.

Mode and details are included in workspace backups and restored with the retained nodes. Archived records remain read-only. Creating another year from a previous year retains simple mode but resets status to Not started and clears operational owner, dates, notes and old workflow details.

在年度项目中选择业务模块，打开「设置所选业务模块 → 模块模式 → 简化模式」。新增模块也可选择此模式；同一年度项目可同时使用完整和简化模块。

简化模块只管理「未开始、进行中、待处理、已完成」、负责人、开始日、截止日及备注，不设节点或完成条件。模块状态可直接修改，其余资料通过「编辑模块资料」修改；模块资料不会改动年度项目的负责人或排期。

切换现有模块时保留原有节点及勾选，切回完整模式即可恢复。简化状态独立记录，默认未开始，不从旧节点推断完成。只有手动选择已完成，才计入已完成模块；Outstanding 不会自动清除。备份保留这些资料，归档后只读，结转新年度时保留模式并清空执行状态及本年度资料。
