# Simple / Pro business modules · 简化 / Pro 业务模块

The **Pro** switch at the top of the left toolbar applies to all active company business modules in this workspace, across companies and years. It is off by default: Simple is the normal experience. Turn it on for the existing detailed stages, completion criteria, full navigation, schedule, reports and template library. This is the only interface-density switch. It does not change holding-company consolidation mode.

Simple modules show one editable row per module: status, owner and due date. Expand More details for start date, notes and module settings. Text and date edits save when leaving the field; Enter saves a single-line field and Escape discards its unsaved draft. Reversed dates are rejected. Not started, In progress, On hold and Completed remain independent of the retained Pro workflow. Only Completed contributes a completed unit; Outstanding remains independent. Existing simple statuses survive toggling; a module without one starts Not started, without inferring completion from old detailed nodes.

Simple home focuses on deadlines, outstanding work and recent visits. Percentages, progress rings, setup prompts and manual-priority cards are omitted; project and company metadata can be expanded when needed. Deadline reminders, outstanding work and Backup/Restore remain available in both modes. Switching Pro off while viewing reports or the full schedule returns to Home.

The workspace choice is saved and included in backups. Old backups without the setting open in Simple; their retained nodes remain available in Pro. Archived companies and years keep their saved mode and data until restored. Newly created modules and annual engagements follow the workspace mode; Simple creates no template nodes. Annual rollover resets operational details and status.

左侧工具栏顶部的 **Pro 开关**统一控制当前工作区所有公司、所有未归档年度的业务模块和界面详略。默认关闭；打开后恢复原有阶段、完成条件、完整导航、排期、报告和范本库。不再另设「简化视图」开关，也不改变控股公司的合并模式。

简化模式直接在模块行内修改状态、负责人和截止日；开始日、备注和模块设置收在「更多资料」。文字和日期离开输入框时保存，单行输入可用 Enter 保存，Escape 撤销当前草稿；日期先后仍须有效。首页保留期限、待清事项和最近访问，收起百分比、进度圈及低频资料。提醒、待清中心和备份恢复始终可用。

原有节点和勾选保留，打开 Pro 即可恢复；两种模式的完成记录各自独立，没有简化状态的旧模块首次显示「未开始」。全局选择随备份保存，归档记录保持原样。关闭 Pro 时，报告或完整排期页会返回首页。
