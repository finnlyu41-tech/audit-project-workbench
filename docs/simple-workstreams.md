# Simple / Pro business modules · 简化 / Pro 业务模块

The **Pro** switch at the top of the left toolbar applies to all active company business modules in this workspace, across companies and years. It is off by default: Simple is the normal experience. Turn it on for the existing detailed stages and completion criteria. There is no separate mode selector in each module. This does not change holding-company consolidation mode or navigation density.

Simple modules track Not started, In progress, On hold and Completed, plus owner, start/due dates and notes. Only Completed contributes a completed unit; Outstanding remains independent. Old nodes and checks remain stored and return in Pro. Existing simple statuses survive toggling; a module without one starts Not started, without inferring completion from old detailed nodes. The Pro workflow and simple status remain independent.

The workspace choice is saved and included in backups. Old backups without the setting open in Simple; their retained nodes remain available in Pro. Archived companies and years keep their saved mode and data until restored. Newly created modules and annual engagements follow the workspace mode; Simple creates no template nodes. Annual rollover resets operational details and status.

左侧工具栏顶部的 **Pro 开关**统一控制当前工作区所有公司、所有未归档年度的业务模块。默认关闭，使用简化模式；打开后使用原有阶段及完成条件。无需逐个模块设置，不改变控股公司的合并模式。

简化模式管理整个模块的状态、负责人、日期和备注。原有节点和勾选保留，打开 Pro 即可恢复；两种模式的完成记录各自独立，没有简化状态的旧模块首次显示「未开始」。未清事项独立保留。全局选择随备份保存，归档记录保持原样。
