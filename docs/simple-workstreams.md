# Simple / Pro business modules · 简化 / Pro 业务模块

The **Pro** switch at the top of the left toolbar applies to all active company business modules in this workspace, across companies and years. It is off by default: Simple is the normal experience. Turn it on for the existing detailed stages, completion criteria, full navigation, schedule, reports and template library. This is the only interface-density switch. It does not change holding-company consolidation mode.

Simple modules use compact rows with shared column labels: module, status and due date. Expand Details for owner, start date, notes and module settings. Project metadata, duplicate and archive actions sit below the module list; the header retains one labelled edit action. Text and date edits save when leaving the field; Enter saves a single-line field and Escape discards its unsaved draft. Reversed dates are rejected. Not started, In progress, On hold and Completed remain independent of the retained Pro workflow. Only Completed contributes a completed unit; Outstanding remains independent. Existing simple statuses survive toggling; a module without one starts Not started, without inferring completion from old detailed nodes.

Select the due date in the Simple project summary to open the existing schedule-only editor, without expanding project metadata or the full annual form. A project without a deadline offers the same shortcut. Only that annual project’s schedule is edited; reporting periods, module deadlines and tax deadlines stay separate. Archived projects and archived companies show dates read-only.

The Simple add/edit form also starts with category, status and due date. Owner, start date, notes and mode explanations sit in Details; a required custom name remains visible. Folding preserves entered and existing values, and an invalid folded field opens its section for correction. Form edits apply only through Add/Save; cancelling a changed draft still asks before discarding it. Pro's template form is unchanged.

Simple navigation opens a flat project list with search and status filters, without a company/project switch. Counts refer to annual engagements; companies without one remain reachable as labelled company rows. Select the company name in a project heading to open its master record. Pro navigation and advanced filters are retained but do not filter Simple results.

Simple home puts deadlines and outstanding work before recent visits. Its filter button sits beside the action-list heading; filters can be folded even when applied, with a visible active-filter label and clear action. Percentages, progress rings, setup prompts and manual-priority cards are omitted; project and company metadata can be expanded when needed. Deadline reminders, outstanding work and Backup/Restore remain available in both modes. Switching Pro off while viewing reports or the full schedule returns to Home.

The workspace choice is saved and included in backups. Old backups without the setting open in Simple; their retained nodes remain available in Pro. Archived companies and years keep their saved mode and data until restored. Newly created modules and annual engagements follow the workspace mode; Simple creates no template nodes. Annual rollover resets operational details and status.

左侧工具栏顶部的 **Pro 开关**统一控制当前工作区所有公司、所有未归档年度的业务模块和界面详略。默认关闭；打开后恢复原有阶段、完成条件、完整导航、排期、报告和范本库。不再另设「简化视图」开关，也不改变控股公司的合并模式。

简化模式以紧凑行显示模块、状态和截止日；负责人、开始日、备注和模块设置收在「详情」。项目资料、复制和归档放在模块清单下方，页首只保留带文字的编辑按钮。文字和日期离开输入框时保存，单行输入可用 Enter 保存，Escape 撤销当前草稿；日期先后仍须有效。首页先显示期限和待清事项，再显示最近访问；筛选按钮并入清单标题，有筛选条件时仍可收起，并保留「筛选中」及清除按钮。百分比、进度圈及低频资料继续收起。提醒、待清中心和备份恢复始终可用。

点击简化项目摘要中的截止日可直接打开排期小窗，不必展开更多资料或打开完整年度项目表。未设置截止日时仍保留入口；只修改该年度项目排期，报告期间、模块截止日和税务期限保持独立，已归档项目或公司只读。

新增／编辑模块表单默认只显示类别、状态和截止日；负责人、开始日、备注及模式说明放进「详情」，必填的自定义名称仍直接显示。折叠不清空已存内容或当前输入；折叠字段校验失败时会自动展开。表单须选择添加／保存才应用更改，取消有修改的草稿仍会先确认。Pro 范本表单不变。

左侧直接列出年度项目，只保留搜索和状态筛选；数字只计算项目，未建项目的公司另以文字行保留入口。点击项目标题下的公司名可打开公司主档。Pro 的导航偏好及高级筛选保留，但不影响简化模式的结果。

原有节点和勾选保留，打开 Pro 即可恢复；两种模式的完成记录各自独立，没有简化状态的旧模块首次显示「未开始」。全局选择随备份保存，归档记录保持原样。关闭 Pro 时，报告或完整排期页会返回首页。
